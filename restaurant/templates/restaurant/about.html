About us

Based in Chicago, Illinois, Little Lemon is a family-owned Mediterranean restaurant focused on traditional recipes served with a modern twist.

Our chefs draw inspiration from Italian, Greek and Turkish cooking, with a menu of 12–15 dishes that rotates with the seasons.

The restaurant is rustic and relaxed with moderate prices, which makes it an easy choice for a meal at any time of day.

Little Lemon is run by brothers Mario and Adrian, who moved to the United States to open a restaurant together. Mario builds the menu from family recipes and his years as a chef in Italy. Adrian runs marketing and pushed the menu beyond classic Italian to include dishes from across the Mediterranean.
